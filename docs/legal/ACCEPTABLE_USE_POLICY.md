# Acceptable Use Policy

**Operator:** [OPERATOR: legal name]  **Effective date:** [OPERATOR: effective date]

ResearchPilot AI sends requests to websites and to external AI and search services, so misuse can harm
other people and the operator. You agree not to:

* Research, collect or compile information to harass, stalk, dox or target an individual, or to
  expose private details about someone who is not a public figure acting in a public role.
* Request or generate content that is illegal where you or the operator are located, that sexualises
  minors, or that gives meaningful help to cause serious physical harm.
* Use the service to produce deceptive material presented as genuine: fake reviews, fabricated
  quotes, forged sources, or reports claiming checks that were not made.
* Attempt to reach private or internal addresses through the page fetcher, or to bypass the safeguards
  that block them (the application refuses private, loopback and link-local addresses).
* Probe, scan, overload or attack the service, other users or any site (including by scripting many
  runs); bypass rate limits, quotas or account restrictions; or access another user's data.
* Upload malware, or content you have no right to process (for example confidential material you
  are not permitted to send to external AI providers).
* Hide an automated agent behind another identity. The fetcher identifies itself as `ResearchPilotBot`;
  do not change that to evade a site's rules.
* Resell or share your account, or use output in a way that breaks the terms of the sources cited.
