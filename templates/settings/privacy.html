Your research

Your history lists every run, and each project page has a delete button that removes that project with its runs, sources, claims and reports. Deleting a project is permanent in the application; copies in operator backups follow the operator's backup schedule.

Download your data

You get one ZIP file with your account details and preferences, projects (including text taken from documents you uploaded), runs, sources, excerpts, claims, reports with their history and quality results, provider usage counts and the audit events that name you. Your password hash and session data are never included. Limited to 5 downloads per day.
